# Approved scope and decisions
- RFQ CRUD, list/search/detail, requested items, mapping, lifecycle, owner assignment, close, audit, OpenAPI and UI.
- Capture original buyer requirements before SKU mapping. Keep immutable original snapshots and append-only requirement revisions.

- Lifecycle: DRAFT -> IN_REVIEW -> QUOTED -> CLOSED, plus CANCELLED. WON/LOST/NO_BID/EXPIRED are close_outcome values.
